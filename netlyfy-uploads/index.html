THE FRY STREET

Crispy Bites.
Bold Flavours.

The Fry Street is a vibrant street-food destination at Gomti Riverfront, Lucknow, created for people who love crispy favourites, bold flavours and food worth coming back for.

From loaded fries and satisfying snacks to flavour-packed bites, every order is made to bring together freshness, taste and indulgence. Whether you are catching up with friends, enjoying an evening by the riverfront or simply craving something delicious, we aim to make every visit feel easy, exciting and memorable.

Located at Shop 9, Food Valley, Gomti Riverfront, The Fry Street combines great food, a lively atmosphere and late-evening convenience in one experience — all built around our promise of being Crispy • Bold • Addictive.
